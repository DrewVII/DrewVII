## 🔭 Featured work

### [DocuSense](https://github.com/DrewVII/DocuSense) · RAG with automatic evaluation
Retrieval-augmented generation with an evaluation harness built in — because a RAG system without measurement is a demo, not a product.

### [Vault](https://github.com/DrewVII/Vault) · Local-first finance tracker with an on-device AI advisor
Minimalist personal finance for macOS, with a built-in financial advisor (Deepseek-R1) and on-device speech input (WhisperKit). Everything runs locally — no data leaves the machine.

`Swift` · `Deepseek-R1` · `WhisperKit` · `on-device inference`

### [Paris AQI Forecasting](https://github.com/DrewVII/Paris-AQI-Forecasting) · 24h air-quality forecasting
A GRU forecasts EPA AQI 24 hours ahead from NASA GEOS-CF reanalysis, with median and 90th-percentile outputs.

- **~30% lower MAE than persistence**, peaking at 32% skill at 12–18h lead
- Residual targets, so the baseline becomes the model's floor rather than its rival
- Quantile (pinball) loss, so the forecast can't hedge low during pollution episodes
- Leakage-checked: chronological splits, train-only scalers, verified window alignment

`PyTorch` · `Google Earth Engine` · `pandas`

### [NLP Sentiment Analysis](https://github.com/DrewVII/NLP-Sentimental-Analysis)
Social media sentiment classification.

`Jupyter` · `NLP`
